About Beauto

Beauto connects customers with verified, independent beauty professionals for makeup, mehndi, hair, skincare, and nail services — delivered at home, for weddings, events, or an everyday touch-up.

Verified beauticians

Every beautician is manually reviewed and approved by our team before they can accept a single order.

Nearest-match booking

We match you with the closest available beautician for your exact date and time — automatically.

Transparent pricing

Package price + a fixed platform fee, shown upfront — no surprise charges at your doorstep.

How it works

  1. Pick a service and package on our Services page.
  2. Choose a date, time, and pin your address.
  3. We match you with the nearest available, verified beautician.
  4. Track your order status from your dashboard — reschedule or cancel any time before it's confirmed.
